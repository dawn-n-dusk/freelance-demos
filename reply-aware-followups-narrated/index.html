Once a customer replies, automated reminders should stop. Here's how this workflow makes that happen.

A new enquiry creates one contact and one follow-up, ready for the team to handle.

When a reply arrives, the contact is marked as replied. Every later reminder checks that status.

A second form doesn't erase the reply. The next reminder is blocked, keeping the conversation natural.
